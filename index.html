01About

I’m Jack — a second-year Engineering Physics student at the University of British Columbia. Before that, I grew up in northern Ontario, where two summers at EXP, a consulting engineering office in New Liskeard, taught me what engineering looks like when it leaves the textbook: drawings with deadlines, clients with questions, and software you learn on a Tuesday because the project needs it Wednesday.

What pulls me in is the overlap of mechanical design, fluids and electronics — a propeller you can hold, a circuit board you can’t quite keep quiet, a water network that has to behave. Right now that shows up as propulsion work on UBC’s human-powered submarine team, where I design and machine parts and occasionally get to watch the boat actually swim.

I’m looking for a summer 2027 internship or research position where I can design real things under real constraints — mechanical, electromechanical, or somewhere in between. If that sounds like your team, get in touch.
